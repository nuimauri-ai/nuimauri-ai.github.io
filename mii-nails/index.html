Why Russian

A standard manicure cleans up to the cuticle. A Russian manicure goes into it.

Dry, with a fine bit, so the product sits right against the skin. That is why the regrowth line takes three weeks to show instead of one, and why it costs a little more.

Book a Russian manicure
i.

Dry prep, no soaking

The skin stays firm so the bit can work clean.

ii.

Lifted, not clipped

The cuticle is lifted and removed with an e-file.

iii.

Laid into the fold

Product right against the skin, then cured under the lamp.
