Why Russian

It's about the fold

A standard manicure cleans up to the cuticle. A Russian manicure goes into it: dry, with a fine bit, so the product sits right against the skin. That's why the regrowth line takes three weeks to show instead of one, and why it costs a little more.

01Dry prep, no soaking. The skin stays firm so the bit can work clean.
02Cuticle lifted and removed with an e-file, not clipped.
03Product laid right into the fold, then cured under the lamp.
Services

Six things we're good at

Short list on purpose. Everything here is done in the salon, by hand, with the lamp times we actually mean.

01

Russian manicure

Dry cuticle work with an e-file, right into the fold. Colour sits flush against the skin and the regrowth line takes twice as long to show.

02

Acrylic & gel extensions

Built to the shape and length you ask for, square, almond, coffin or stiletto, thin at the free edge so they don't feel like someone else's hands.

03

Nail art

French tips, chrome, 3D flowers, pearls and charms. Bring a photo or describe it and we'll tell you honestly what will hold up.

04

Manicure & pedicure

Shape, tidy, and a polish or gel. Combo bookings if you want both done in one sitting. Men's pedicures too, no polish unless you ask.

05

Lashes

Classic, hybrid, wispy, cat eye or doll eye, mapped to your eye shape. Infills booked before you leave so you're never caught out with gaps.

06

Waxing & brows

Brow shaping and tinting, and face and body waxing while you're in the chair.
