The Experience

An Elevated Escape Above Brookvale

Perched high above Brookvale, The Azure Rooftop Bar offers an unparalleled escape with breathtaking 360-degree views of the district and the coastal horizon. Discover a world where innovative cocktails, exquisite small plates, and a vibrant atmosphere converge to create unforgettable moments.

The Space

Industrial Elegance Meets Coastal Charm

Our interior seamlessly blends exposed industrial beams and copper fixtures with plush navy velvet seating and warm timber flooring. Floor-to-ceiling glass frames the coastal skyline, creating an atmosphere that transitions effortlessly from afternoon relaxation to evening sophistication.
