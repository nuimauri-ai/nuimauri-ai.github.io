Services

Six services, one chair

Everything on this list is done in the shop by the same hands. Nothing gets rushed because the next booking is waiting.

Skin fades

Taken as high or as low as you want, blended out so there's no line where the clipper stopped.

Beard shaping

Shaped to your face, lined with a razor, and left long enough that it still looks right in two weeks.

Face waxing

Ears, nose, cheekbones and neckline. Two minutes, and it makes more difference than most people expect.

Threading

Precise work on brows and around the eyes, without the redness you get from wax on thin skin.

Facials

Cleanse, steam, extraction and a mask. Book it with a cut and you're out in under an hour.

Kids cuts

Patient with the ones who won't sit still, and quick with the ones who'd rather be anywhere else.
