The problem

Everyone's mum shops there.

Symptom

Ageing customer

The loyal shopper is 45+. Gen Z sees "DJs" as where their parents buy work shirts and gifts.

Symptom

Losing to online

Marketplaces, brand websites and fast fashion take the sales. The department store keeps the costs.

Opening

Turnaround is funded

New app, refurbished flagships, Qantas loyalty deal. Costs are fixed. Young customers are the missing piece.

Say thisThe brand lost 95% of its value between 2014 and 2023 and lost $95.5 million last year. The turnaround is working on costs, but every customer they win back is one they already had. You cannot cost-cut your way to a new generation.

3

The idea

Turn old into the hook.

Campaign platform

Since 1838.
Yours now.

Being 188 years old is the one thing Zara, Shein and Amazon can never copy. Treat the archive like a streetwear drop and the store like a venue.

  • DJ Archive. Monthly reissues from 188 years of catalogues, app-only for 48 hours.
  • DJ Pre-Loved. Designer resale floor in the flagship. Trade in for Qantas points.
  • The 7th Floor. Top floor of Elizabeth St becomes a filmable space with Thursday night events.
  • First Wardrobe. Free 30-minute styling session for 18 to 26s.
Say thisOne line: Since 1838, yours now. Stop apologising for being old and sell the archive like a drop. Four things to do: Archive drops, Pre-Loved, the 7th Floor, First Wardrobe.
4

Media plan

POEM: Paid, Owned, Earned

Each one has a job. Paid buys the first look. Owned turns it into an account. Earned gets other people to do the selling.

Paid 50% of budget

Reach people who have never thought about DJ.

  • TikTok Spark Ads boosting creator videos, not brand ads.
  • 12 Australian creators on 3-month contracts.
  • Posters and audio around train stations and unis.

Owned 20% of budget

Turn attention into an app account and a visit.

  • Archive drops app-only for 48 hours.
  • Double Qantas points for under-30s.
  • DJ's own TikTok: behind-the-archive videos and staff styling.

Earned 30% of budget

Make people talk about David Jones again.

  • #DJArchive fit checks and trade-in unboxings (UGC).
  • Press story: "188-year-old store opens its archive to Gen Z".
  • Thursday night events with young labels and a Depop collab.
PAID 50%OWNED 20%EARNED 30%

Example: Maya sees a creator's video (Paid) → downloads the app for the drop (Owned) → posts her fit check to 400 friends (Earned).

Say thisPaid: we buy reach for creator content, not polished ads. Owned: the app gets the drop first and Qantas points are doubled. Earned: fit checks, press, events. Half the money is Paid at launch, but as Earned grows, Paid shrinks. Owned is what David Jones keeps forever.
