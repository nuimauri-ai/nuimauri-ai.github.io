Why this offer

It fixes the three things that keep Gen Z out.

Barrier

"I don't know what to buy."

First job, graduation, first wedding guest. They have the occasion and the money, not the confidence. A stylist removes the fear.

Barrier

"It's not for me."

David Jones feels like their parents' store. A free, personal, no-pressure session says the opposite: we made time for you.

Barrier

"Why would I go in?"

Nothing online gives them a reason to visit. A booked appointment is a reason, a date and a time.

Why DJ can win

It already has the stylists

Personal shopping exists today, but only for high spenders. We open the door to the next generation.

Why DJ can win

It has the brands and the floors

One session can show Country Road, Nike, Aje and a $90 shirt in the same hour. No single-brand store can.

Why DJ can win

It costs almost nothing

Stylist time on quiet Thursday nights and Saturdays. The media budget does the heavy lifting, not the offer.

Media plan

POEM: fill the slots.

Every channel has one job: get a booking. Paid finds them, Owned books them, Earned makes their friends book too.

Paid 55% of budget

Find 18 to 26s at their "first" moment.

  • TikTok Spark Ads on creators' session videos, with a "Book free" button.
  • TikTok and Google search ads: "what to wear first job interview", "wedding guest outfit Sydney".
  • Instagram and Snapchat ads geo-targeted to unis, CBD offices, graduation season.
  • 10 creators paid to book, film and post their session.

Owned 15% of budget

Make booking effortless and keep them after.

  • Booking lives in the DJ app: 30-second flow, occasion and budget picked up front.
  • DJ's own TikTok: "30 minutes with a stylist" series, one real session per week.
  • Email and SMS to Qantas members under 30, in-store signage, stylists as the faces of the campaign.
  • After the session: looks saved in the app, reminder at 7 days, birthday re-book.

Earned 30% of budget

Turn every session into the next booking.

  • #FirstWardrobe before-and-after posts, reposted by DJ.
  • Bring a friend: refer someone and you both get a second session.
  • 100 micro-creators gifted a session, no obligation to post.
  • Press: "David Jones gives Gen Z a free stylist." Uni societies and grad programs as partners.
PAID 55%OWNED 15%EARNED 30%

Proposed split of the 12-week media budget. Paid is heavy at launch and shrinks as referrals and creator posts take over.

Three phases

Weeks 1 to 2 · Pilot

Two flagships, 10 creators booked first, app flow live, stylists trained on a 30-minute format and three outfits per budget.

Weeks 3 to 6 · Launch

Paid at full weight, search ads on, press story out, Thursday nights extended to 9pm. Target 1,000 bookings a week.

Weeks 7 to 12 · Scale

Roll out to all 38 stores, bring-a-friend on, Paid shifts to boosting customers' own videos, graduation-season push.
