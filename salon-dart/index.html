II The one people travel for

Japanese straightening is permanent. That is the whole point of it, and why it is not something to book casually.

Bio ionic straightening and rebonding break the bonds in the hair and reset them flat. New growth comes in with your natural texture, so the line has to be managed properly at every retouch.

It's structural

Once done, it stays done. We plan the retouch schedule with you on the first day.

It takes time

Depending on length and density, allow most of a day. We would rather block out too much than rush the neutralising stage.

Consultation first

Bleached, previously rebonded or heavily damaged hair may not be a candidate. We tell you in the chair, before you have paid for anything.
